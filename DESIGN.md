# Allian visual contract

The approved screenshots in `DesignReference/` are the visual contract.

The running app must use approximately equal amounts of pink, purple and blue, with soft swirly pastel gradients, rounded white/glass panels, large child-friendly controls, hearts/clouds/rainbows and colourful world art.

The actual app must visibly include the same themes shown in the screenshots: avatar customisation including crop tops, world/town exploration, houses and pool, mall and shops, zoo and aquarium, airport/train travel, mini phone, food delivery, friends/live-together and 100+ mini-games.

A plain black/white SwiftUI `List`, default grey form controls, coin/XP counters, leaderboards or locked-item storefronts violate the approved design.
